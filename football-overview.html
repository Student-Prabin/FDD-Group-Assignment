About the club

The APU Falcons Football Club was formed to give students a competitive outlet outside the classroom and has grown into the university's most-followed sport, competing in the Malaysian University Football League (MUFL) inter-varsity division.

Achievements

Runners-up, MUFL Division 2 (2024); Semi-finalists, APU Founder's Cup (2025)
