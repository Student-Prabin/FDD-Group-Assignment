About Us

Student-run. Student-led.

Our Mission

APU Sports Hub exists to give every student a route into competitive and recreational sport, regardless of prior experience. We believe university sport builds discipline, teamwork and friendships that outlast a degree.

Our History

What began in 2019 as informal five-a-side football on the rooftop court has grown into four registered clubs competing in national inter-varsity leagues, backed by a fully student-run committee structure.

Scope

This website covers all four flagship clubs — Football, Basketball, Swimming and Tennis — including rosters, fixtures, results, galleries and how to join.
