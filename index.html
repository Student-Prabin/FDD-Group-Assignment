Why We Exist

Sport built by students, for students.

APU Sports Hub is run entirely by student committees across four clubs. Every fixture, training session and highlight reel on this site is organised by your coursemates — not the university administration.

Whether you're chasing a varsity title or just want a reason to leave the library on a Wednesday evening, there's a club here for you.
