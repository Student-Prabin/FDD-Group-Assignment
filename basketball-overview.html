About the club

APU Hoopers was established as a 3-on-3 street league before expanding into a full 5-on-5 program. The team now trains four nights a week and represents APU in the Malaysia Varsity Basketball Championship.

Achievements

Champions, Varsity 3x3 Invitational (2025); Final 8, MVBC (2024)
